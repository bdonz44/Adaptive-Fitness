# Hybrid 12 Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 3, 2026*

Hybrid 12 is a personal training app for iPhone. It is not sold or offered to the public.

## What the app stores
- Your training plan, logged workouts, lift weights, body weight, waist measurements and cardio minutes.
- This data is stored on your iPhone and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account so it syncs between your devices. The app's developer cannot see it.

## WHOOP data
- If you connect WHOOP, the app reads your recovery, sleep, cycle (strain) and workout data from the WHOOP API.
- It uses this data only to set your daily readiness and adjust your training suggestions inside the app.
- WHOOP data is kept on your device. It is never sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
- You can disconnect at any time in the app, or remove access from your WHOOP account.

## Apple Health
- With your permission, the app saves finished workouts to Apple Health and may read workouts, sleep and heart rate from it. Health data stays on your device and in Apple's systems.

## What the app does not do
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no data sold or shared with third parties.

## Deleting your data
- Delete the app, or use Plan > Start over, to erase your training data. iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud.

## Contact
Questions: contact the app's owner directly.
